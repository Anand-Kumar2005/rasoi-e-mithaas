Gulab Jamun

🧺 Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Milk Powder
  • Maida (All-purpose flour)
  • Baking Soda
  • Ghee / Oil
  • Water
  • Cardamom Powder

🍲 Recipe

  • Mix milk powder, maida and baking soda.
  • Add ghee and make a soft dough.
  • Make small balls without cracks.
  • Deep fry on low flame until golden.
  • Prepare sugar syrup with cardamom.
  • Soak fried balls in warm syrup for 1 to 2 hours.

Barfi

🧺 Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Milk Powder
  • Milk
  • Cardamom Powder
  • Ghee
  • Cardamom Powder

🍲 Recipe

  • Boil milk in a heavy bottom pan.
  • Add milk powder and stir continuously.
  • Add sugar and cook until thick.
  • Add ghee and cardamom powder.
  • Pour in a greased tray and set.
  • Cut into pieces and garnish with nuts.

Jalebi

🧺 Ingredients

  • Maida
  • Curd
  • Food Color (optional)
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Lemon Juice
  • Oil / Ghee

🍲 Recipe

  • Make a smooth batter and ferment overnight.
  • Prepare sugar syrup (one string consistency).
  • Heat oil in a pan.
  • Pour batter in a circular motion.
  • Fry until crispy and golden.
  • Dip in warm sugar syrup for a minute.

Rasmalai

🧺 Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Lemon Juice / Vinegar
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom Powder
  • Saffron
  • Pistachios

🍲 Recipe

  • Boil milk and curdle it with lemon juice.
  • Strain and knead to make soft chenna.
  • Make small balls and boil in sugar water.
  • Prepare thickened milk with sugar.
  • Add cardamom and saffron.
  • Soak chenna balls in chilled milk.
